    Questions

    Good to know

    How much will the puja cost?

    Charges depend on the puja, the date, the venue and whether samagri is included. We share the complete charges and inclusions on WhatsApp before you confirm anything.

    Is a pandit available on my date?

    We check with pandits in your city (Patna, Gurugram or Bengaluru) after we receive your enquiry. We are expanding to more cities soon. Availability for your date and location is confirmed personally. Sending the form does not reserve a pandit.

    Who arranges the samagri?

    You choose in the form: we can arrange it, you can arrange it yourself, or you can ask us for guidance on what is needed. We confirm what is included when we share the charges.

    Can I choose the language of the puja?

    Yes, you can tell us your preference — Hindi, Sanskrit or no preference. We will try to match it, subject to pandit availability.

    When is my booking confirmed?

    Only after we have discussed your requirements and the charges with you on WhatsApp and you have agreed. Until then, your request is an enquiry.

    Can I follow the Pooja Vidhi guide instead of booking a pandit?

    The guides are for learning and preparation. They do not replace a qualified pandit, and your family’s own tradition always comes first. If you want a pandit to conduct the puja, send an enquiry above.

    How reliable are the guides?

    Each step shows the source it comes from, and anything we could not source is marked as pending rather than filled in. Every guide currently says “Draft — Pandit Review Needed”: no pandit has reviewed them yet, and practice differs between regions and families.

    Do I need to log in or pay online?

    No. There is no account and no online payment on this page. You send us your enquiry on WhatsApp and we take it from there.
